Research

Research areas

My research develops cryptographic protocols and privacy-enhancing technologies for settings in which data holders, model owners, participants or infrastructure cannot all be trusted. The work considers formal security and privacy guarantees, efficiency and applications in AI, digital finance and distributed systems.

Precise threat modelsProvable guaranteesPractical efficiency
01

Secure and privacy-preserving AI

How can organisations learn from sensitive or distributed data without exposing records, models or intermediate information?

I develop privacy-preserving federated learning, private inference and secure collaborative-learning methods. The work combines cryptographic protocols with systems design to address leakage, malicious participants, constrained devices, data quality and verifiability.

  • Federated learning
  • Private inference
  • Data quality
  • Secure collaboration

02

Cryptography and privacy-enhancing technologies

How can parties compute, verify or exchange information when they do not trust one another?

I design and analyse protocols for secure multi-party computation, private set intersection, oblivious transfer, verifiable computation, time-lock techniques and post-quantum settings. The emphasis is on explicit assumptions, provable security and usable performance.

  • Secure computation
  • Oblivious transfer
  • Time-lock puzzles
  • Post-quantum protocols

03

Digital finance and trustworthy infrastructure

How can payments, financial collaboration and decentralised systems remain secure, accountable and privacy-preserving?

I work on secure payment protocols, fraud-resistant mechanisms, blockchain and smart-contract security, insured cryptocurrency transactions, and privacy-preserving approaches to financial fraud detection.

  • Secure payments
  • Fraud protection
  • Blockchain systems
  • Financial data collaboration
