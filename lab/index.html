Cryptography and AI Security Lab

Cryptography and AI Security Lab

We study cryptographic protocols and privacy-enhancing technologies for secure AI, digital finance, blockchain systems and distributed infrastructure.

Based at Newcastle University and led by Dr Aydin Abadi.

Our mission

Research aims

Many systems involve data, models or participants that cannot all be trusted. We design and analyse cryptographic protocols for computation and collaboration in these settings.

Our work connects formal security and privacy guarantees with practical requirements in AI, finance, payments, blockchain and distributed infrastructure.
