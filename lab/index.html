Cryptography and AI Security Lab

Cryptography and AI Security Lab

We design and analyse cryptographic protocols for private computation, private data access and verifiable digital exchange. Our work includes cryptanalysis, formal security analysis, implementation and measured evaluation.

Based at Newcastle University and led by Dr Aydin Abadi.

Our mission

Research aims

We study what can be computed, retrieved or exchanged when data, models or participants cannot all be trusted.

The programme covers privacy-preserving computation and learning, private access and verifiable computation, financial cryptography, and cryptanalysis. We state the assumptions and leakage of each protocol and evaluate implementation costs where appropriate.

Research themes

Current research areas

Four related lines of work connect protocol design, cryptanalysis, formal security and implementation.

01

Privacy-preserving computation and learning

Secure computation, federated learning and private inference across sensitive or distributed data.

02

Private access and verifiable computation

Oblivious transfer, private set intersection, time-lock techniques, verifiable outsourcing and post-quantum protocols.

03

Financial cryptography and secure digital exchange

Cryptographic protocols for payments, fraud detection, disputes and financial data collaboration.

04

Cryptanalysis and protocol security

Analysis of proposed constructions, security proofs and protocol failures, together with mitigations where possible.
