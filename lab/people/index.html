People

Researchers and collaborators working across cryptography, secure AI, privacy-enhancing technologies, digital finance and trustworthy distributed systems.

Leadership

Lab director

Director

Dr Aydin Abadi

Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in Cybersecurity at Newcastle University.

Aydin studies cryptographic protocols and privacy-enhancing technologies for AI, digital finance and distributed systems. He leads the lab’s research programme and supervises its doctoral researchers.

Doctoral researchers

Current researchers

Current doctoral projects in cryptography, privacy-preserving machine learning and distributed systems.

Doctoral researcher · 2025-present

Hamed Alghamdi

Hamed develops privacy-preserving federated learning for resource-constrained devices. His research examines how clients with limited computation, memory or energy can participate in delegated training without exposing private data, using tools including homomorphic encryption and secure computation.

Doctoral researcher · 2025-present

Jakub Kacper Szelag

Jakub works across cryptography, AI security and distributed computation. His research focuses on attacks against distributed machine-learning systems and on verifiable, privacy-preserving methods, including applications in healthcare.

Doctoral researcher · 2026-present

Ishak Beno

Ishak develops privacy-preserving data-cleaning methods for federated learning. He studies how distributed datasets can be made more consistent and reliable without revealing sensitive records, with applications in healthcare and financial services.

Research practice

How we work

Define the guarantee

Make the trust assumptions, adversary and leakage explicit.

Build evidence

Support formal analysis with implementations, measurements and stated limitations where appropriate.

Work across boundaries

Translate between cryptographic theory, systems requirements and domain needs.
