General PhD enquiries

If no advertised studentship matches your circumstances, you may send a focused enquiry. Please include:

  • Curriculum vitae
  • Academic transcript
  • A short statement of research interests
  • One paragraph explaining the fit with a named lab theme

A general enquiry is not an offer of admission or funding.

Postdoctoral and visiting researchers

Enquiries are welcome when there is a clear connection to the lab’s programme. Please identify the intended research question, likely funding route, proposed period and any existing outputs that establish the fit.
