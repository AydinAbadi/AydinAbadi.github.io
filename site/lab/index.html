Cryptography and AI Security Lab

Cryptographic assurance for AI and digital finance

We develop cryptographic and privacy-preserving technologies that protect data, models, policies and digital infrastructure while preserving utility, accountability and verifiability.

Based at Newcastle University and led by Dr Aydin Abadi.

Our mission

Security that survives contact with the real system

Many valuable systems depend on data, models or participants that cannot simply be trusted. We build and analyse cryptographic systems that make secure collaboration possible in those settings.

Our work connects formal security and privacy guarantees with practical requirements in AI, finance, payments, blockchain and distributed infrastructure.

Industry and public-sector engagement

Start with the system and threat model

We work with researchers, companies and public-sector organisations facing difficult security and privacy problems in AI, data collaboration, payments, blockchain and digital infrastructure.

How to collaborate

A useful first brief

  1. What system or decision are you trying to protect?
  2. Which data, models or assets must remain confidential?
  3. Who may be malicious, compromised or simply untrusted?
  4. What evidence, prototype or outcome would be useful?
