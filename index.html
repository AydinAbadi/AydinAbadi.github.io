ACM CCS 2026 · Verifiable quantum computation

Time-Delayed Publicly Verifiable Quantum Computation with Classical Verifiers

A time-delayed construction for publicly verifiable delegated quantum computation. It avoids the indistinguishability-obfuscation machinery used in earlier approaches by combining commitment schemes with time-lock puzzles; security is proved in the quantum random-oracle model with a common reference string.

Read the preprint View CCS acceptance

PETS 2026 · Delegated private retrieval

Oblivis

A framework for delegated oblivious transfer under a non-collusion assumption. Its Supersonic OT implementation achieves at least a 92× speedup over a highly efficient 1-out-of-2 OT and completes an end-to-end transfer in 1.36 ms on a Raspberry Pi 4.

Read the paper

Financial crime · Federated learning

STARLIT

A scalable privacy-preserving federated-learning system for financial fraud detection. STARLIT was a joint first-place winner in the UK-US Privacy-Enhancing Technologies Prize Challenge.

NDSS 2025 · Private data quality

Private data deduplication for federated learning

A privacy-preserving protocol for deduplicating records across distributed datasets, evaluated for training efficiency and language-model quality.
