Biography

Research profile

Dr Aydin Abadi is a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in Cybersecurity in Newcastle University’s School of Computing and Director of the Cryptography and AI Security Lab.

His research examines how useful computation can remain secure when data, models, participants or infrastructure cannot be fully trusted. He designs and analyses cryptographic protocols and privacy-enhancing technologies for secure AI, collaborative machine learning, digital finance and decentralised systems.

His research includes secure multi-party computation, private set intersection, oblivious transfer, verifiable computation, time-lock techniques, post-quantum cryptography, secure payments and blockchain security. The work combines formal analysis with implementation and experimental evaluation where appropriate.

His team was a joint first-place winner in the UK-US Privacy-Enhancing Technologies Prize Challenge for STARLIT. His research has appeared or been accepted at venues including ACM CCS, NDSS, PETS, AsiaCCS and IEEE EuroS&P.
